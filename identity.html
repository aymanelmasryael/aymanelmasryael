Section 02

Logo System

The AEL logo is a modular geometric mark. It is never redrawn, never reinterpreted, never stylized.

Logo Meaning

The logo is built from a 7x7 square grid. The top segment forms the letter "A" through negative space — a curved arch carved from a solid block. The bottom segment is a matrix of 4x4 blue squares representing structured intelligence, modular systems, and computational precision. The mark reads as both an architectural form and a digital signature.

Primary Logo

Dark background  ·  Blue background  ·  Light background

Safe Area & Minimum Size

The safe area equals 50% of the logo height on all four sides. No text, graphic, or edge may enter this zone. Minimum render size: 24px in height for digital, 8mm for print.

Correct vs Incorrect Usage

DO
Use only the approved SVG file. Maintain aspect ratio. Apply on black, white, or AEL Blue backgrounds. Scale proportionally.
DO NOT
Stretch, rotate, or skew the logo. Apply drop shadows, gradients, or borders. Change the blue squares to another color. Place on busy backgrounds. Redraw or reinterpret the mark.

OverlaysSubtle blue gradient overlays allowed for hero sections. Opacity max 15%.

Aspect ratios16:9 (hero), 4:3 (cards), 1:1 (avatars).

AI-Generated Image Guidelines

When generating images via AI (Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion), adhere to these constraints: dark environments, geometric compositions, blue/neutral palette, no text within images, no faces unless professional context, no fantasy or decorative elements. Prompt structure: [subject] · dark environment · minimal composition · geometric lines · blue accent · professional · 8k · architectural photography

Section 07

Voice

The linguistic layer of the brand. How AEL speaks across every channel, platform, and medium.

Tone Principles

ProfessionalSpeak as an expert. No casual language.
TechnicalPrefer precise terminology. Assume an intelligent audience.
MinimalShort sentences. Remove filler words. One idea per sentence.
Evidence-basedSupport claims. Cite data when possible. No vague superlatives.
ConfidentDirect statements. No hedging. No "we believe" — state as fact.
EducationalTeach through communication. Explain the "why" not just the "what."

Writing Rules

DO
Use short paragraphs (2–3 sentences max). Write in active voice. Use precise technical vocabulary. Lead with the conclusion. Use numerals for data: "reduced latency by 40%."
DO NOT
Use marketing hype: "revolutionary," "game-changing," "unprecedented." Use emojis. Use slang or internet abbreviations. Write in passive voice. Use exclamation marks. Exaggerate claims.

Examples

Correct
AEL Digital Studio builds AI-driven design infrastructure for engineering teams. The platform reduces visual asset production time by 60% through automated design tokens and intelligent layout systems.
Incorrect
We're super excited to announce our amazing new AI design platform that will revolutionize the way you create stunning visuals in record time! It's a game-changer for creative teams everywhere.

AI Prompt Rules

When instructing AI systems (Canva AI, ChatGPT, Claude) to generate content for AEL, always include this block at the start of every prompt:

/* AEL AI Prompt Context — Inject at session start */ Brand: AEL Digital Studio™ Founder: Ayman Elmasry · AI Orchestrator · Brand Designer Voice: Professional · Minimal · Technical · Evidence-based Colors: #000000 | #0074FF | #6C47FF | #00D4AA | #FF4D8D | #F59E0B Typography: Inter (heading + body) · JetBrains Mono (code) Design: Swiss minimal · Dark mode · Grid-based · No decorations NO: emojis · slang · exclamation marks · hype · passive voice
